A single operational platform unifying emergency assistance, claims, providers, cost containment, and partner reporting — driven by AI and ready for SOC 2-grade environments.

Product in 90 seconds

Sentinel Travel Ops is a unified operations platform for travel insurance and global assistance teams. Inbound from 7 channels — voice, WhatsApp, SMS, email, app, portal, partner APIs — lands in a single workspace where AI classifies, scores, and routes it in under a second. Low-value claims auto-adjudicate; complex cases reach specialists with full context. Cost containment reads every hospital invoice line-by-line against regional benchmarks, preventing leakage before payment. Predictive risk forecasts surge volume during disruptions and recommends staffing moves. Partners and leadership see live program-level dashboards with SOC 2, ISO 27001, and GDPR controls baked in. Every action is audit-logged forever and replayable on demand.
